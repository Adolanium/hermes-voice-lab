# Storylines

**Recommended: A — "Ask Hermes for a song"** (a stranger follows it: you ask, she sings, you poke her tongue, she performs).

## A. Ask Hermes for a song (picked)
Device: the feature as the hook, then one viewer doing one real task. Form: object-to-object chain.
The film opens on her mid-scat ("shoo bop shoo bop BAH!"); the stab floods the frame Hermes blue and the title's last word becomes Hermes Agent's own wordmark. In the real app you ask for a silly song, the agent writes it into Voice Lab, she sings it live, you seal her tongue and the band stops dead, open her nose and she hums; the camera dives through her face into the full lounge act; the end card.
- First 2 s: her portrait mid-scat, floating words, drum pickup into "BAH!".
- Beats: cold open → title flood → ask → she sings in the app → seal / hum → dive in → the act → end card.
- Look: the app's own white ground, ink #16151a, Hermes blue #2152f4, Collapse Bold (the app's wordmark face), Inter; her ink illustration.
- Sound: an original lounge band scored from her melody: vamp under the app, joins her in-app verse on its downbeat, stops at the seal, the act's own backing, a rimshot, a final chord.

## B. The result first
Device: the result first. Form: one continuous pull-back.
The lounge act plays full-frame; the camera pulls back to reveal it running inside Hermes Desktop, then rewinds to how it got there (the chat, the tools).

## C. Inside her mouth (bold)
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: an endless zoom.
Start on the Hermes chat bubble; dive through the audio card into her portrait, through her lips into the airway, into the vocal folds, out through the glottal pulse into the act.
